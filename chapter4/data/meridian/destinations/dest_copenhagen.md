## Overview
Copenhagen is a flat, cyclable city known for design, harbour baths, and the
Tivoli gardens.

## Best time to visit
Peak season is June to August. April, May, and September are pleasant and less
crowded.

## Getting around
The city runs on bikes; rentals and city bikes are everywhere. The metro is
driverless and runs 24 hours.

## Top things to do
- Stroll Nyhavn's colourful harbour front.
- Ride Tivoli Gardens, one of the oldest amusement parks.
- Swim at an outdoor harbour bath in summer.
- Visit the design museum and the Round Tower.
- Day-trip to the Louisiana modern-art museum up the coast.
