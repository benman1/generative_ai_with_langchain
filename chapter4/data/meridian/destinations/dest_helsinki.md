## Overview
Helsinki is a seafront capital of islands, saunas, and clean design, with ferry
links to the Suomenlinna sea fortress and to Tallinn.

## Best time to visit
The best months are June to August for the archipelago and terraces. Winter is
cold and dark but good for saunas and Christmas markets.

## Getting around
Trams cover the centre; a day ticket includes the Suomenlinna ferry.

## Top things to do
- Ferry to the Suomenlinna sea fortress.
- See the Rock Church (Temppeliaukio) carved into stone.
- Book a traditional public sauna session.
- Browse the Design District shops.
- Take a day ferry to Tallinn, Estonia.
