## Overview
Kyoto is Japan's old imperial capital, dense with temples, shrines, and
traditional wooden streets.

## Best time to visit
The peak season is spring (late March to April) for cherry blossom and autumn
(November) for the maple leaves. Both book out early. Summer is hot and humid.

## Getting around
Buses and two subway lines cover the city; a prepaid IC card works on all of
them. Many sights are walkable from the Higashiyama district.

## Top things to do
- Walk the vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari.
- See the golden pavilion, Kinkaku-ji.
- Stroll the Arashiyama bamboo grove.
- Explore Gion, the traditional geisha district.
- Visit Kiyomizu-dera temple at sunset.
