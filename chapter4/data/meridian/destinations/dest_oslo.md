## Overview
Oslo sits at the head of a fjord, ringed by forest, and is the gateway to
Norway's fjord country.

## Best time to visit
The best months are May to September for fjord trips and long daylight. Winter
suits skiing and the museums.

## Getting around
Trams, buses, the metro, and ferries to the islands all run on one ticket. The
airport express train reaches the centre in about 20 minutes.

## Top things to do
- Visit the Vigeland sculpture park.
- See the Fram polar-expedition ship and the Viking Ship museum.
- Walk the Opera House roof down to the waterfront.
- Take a fjord sightseeing cruise from Aker Brygge.
- Ride up to Holmenkollen for the ski jump and city view.

## Good to know
Norway is not in the EU but is in Schengen and the currency is the krone.
