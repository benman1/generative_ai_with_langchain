## Overview
Reykjavik is the compact capital of Iceland and the base for the Golden Circle,
the Blue Lagoon, and the south-coast waterfalls.

## Best time to visit
Peak season is June to August, with near-midnight sun and every road open.
The shoulder months of May and September are quieter and cheaper. For the
northern lights, come between late September and March.

## Getting around
The city centre is walkable. For day trips you need a rental car or a coach
excursion; there is no train network.

## Top things to do
- Soak in the Blue Lagoon geothermal spa.
- Drive the Golden Circle: Thingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss.
- Walk the harbour and see the Sun Voyager sculpture.
- Climb the Hallgrimskirkja church tower for a city view.
- Take a whale-watching boat from the old harbour.
- Day-trip to the south coast for black-sand beaches and waterfalls.

## Where to eat
The old harbour has well-regarded seafood restaurants. Try a seafood soup by
the harbour. Book a table for seafood near the harbour in peak season.
