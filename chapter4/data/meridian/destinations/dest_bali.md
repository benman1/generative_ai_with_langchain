## Overview
Bali is an Indonesian island of rice terraces, temples, surf beaches, and
resort towns from Seminyak to Ubud.

## Best time to visit
The best months are the dry season, April to October. The wet season, November
to March, is hot with afternoon downpours.

## Getting around
There is no public transport network to speak of; use ride-hailing apps, a
private driver, or a scooter. Traffic is heavy in the south.

## Top things to do
- See the Tegallalang rice terraces near Ubud.
- Visit the Uluwatu clifftop temple at sunset.
- Take a sunrise hike up Mount Batur.
- Learn to surf at Kuta or Canggu.
- Day-trip to the Nusa islands for snorkelling.
