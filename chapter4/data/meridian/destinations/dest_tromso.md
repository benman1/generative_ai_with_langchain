## Overview
Tromso sits well inside the Arctic Circle and is one of the most reliable places
in Europe to see the aurora.

## Best time to visit
For the northern lights, come between late September and early April, when the
nights are dark. The polar night runs from late November to mid-January. For the
midnight sun and hiking, come May to July.

## Getting around
The centre is small and walkable. Aurora trips and whale safaris run as guided
excursions.

## Top things to do
- Join an evening northern-lights chase by minibus.
- Ride the Fjellheisen cable car for an aurora viewpoint.
- Take a winter whale-watching boat (November to January).
- Visit the Arctic Cathedral and the Polaria aquarium.
- Try a dog-sledding or reindeer-sledding excursion.
