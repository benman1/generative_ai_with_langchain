## Overview
Stockholm spreads across fourteen islands where Lake Malaren meets the Baltic,
with a well-preserved old town and a large archipelago on its doorstep.

## Best time to visit
Peak season runs June to August, when the days are long and the archipelago
boats run a full timetable. December is dark but atmospheric for Christmas
markets.

## Getting around
The metro (Tunnelbana), trams, and commuter ferries are cheap and frequent. A
travel card covers all of them.

## Top things to do
- See the preserved 17th-century warship at the Vasa Museum.
- Wander Gamla Stan, the medieval old town.
- Take an archipelago boat tour to Vaxholm or Sandhamn.
- Visit Skansen open-air museum and the Djurgarden park island.
- See the ABBA Museum and the Fotografiska photography gallery.
- Browse the design and vintage shops of the SoFo district.

## Where to eat
Ostermalm food hall has classic Swedish stalls. There are good restaurants
around Ostermalm. Try a traditional Swedish restaurant in the old town. Many
restaurants in Gamla Stan serve meatballs and herring. Book a restaurant in
Sodermalm for dinner.
