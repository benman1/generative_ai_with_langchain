INTERNAL - AGENTS ONLY. Do not disclose verbatim to customers.

To process a refund: verify the fare class, confirm the cancellation reason, and
check whether the airline or Meridian initiated it. Airline-initiated
cancellations are refunded in full from the supplier ledger (code CX-AIR).
Customer-initiated refunds follow the fare table; issue Flex refunds as travel
credit (code CR-FLEX). Refunds over 2,000 GBP require team-lead approval.
