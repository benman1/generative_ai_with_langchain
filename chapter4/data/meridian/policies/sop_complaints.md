INTERNAL - AGENTS ONLY. Do not disclose verbatim to customers.

Acknowledge within 24 hours. Log the case reference. For service failures,
offer the standard goodwill ladder: apology, then travel credit, then partial
refund. Escalate safety, discrimination, or legal-threat cases to the duty
manager immediately. Never admit liability in writing.
