If Meridian or the airline cancels your flight, you are entitled to a full
refund or a free rebooking, and you may be entitled to statutory compensation
under UK/EU air passenger rights depending on the cause and notice given.

If you cancel, what you get back depends on your fare class (see fare
conditions). Basic fares are non-refundable. Flex fares are refunded as travel
credit minus a change fee. Premium fares are fully refundable up to 14 days
before departure. Travel-insurance claims are separate.
