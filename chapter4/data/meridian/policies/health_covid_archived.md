[ARCHIVED 2021 NOTICE - SUPERSEDED]

Entry to Iceland, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, and Finland requires proof of
full COVID-19 vaccination or a negative PCR test taken within 72 hours of
departure. Unvaccinated travellers must quarantine on arrival. A passenger
locator form must be completed before travel.

This notice reflects rules in force in 2021 and is retained for reference only.
