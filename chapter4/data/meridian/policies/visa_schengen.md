Iceland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland are all in the Schengen area.

UK, US, Canadian, Australian, and most EU passport holders do not need a visa
for tourism. You may stay up to 90 days in any rolling 180-day period across the
whole Schengen area combined, not per country.

From 2025 the EU is phasing in ETIAS, a paid online travel authorisation for
visa-exempt visitors. Check whether ETIAS is required for your travel dates.
Always confirm the current rule for your nationality with the official
government source before travelling.
