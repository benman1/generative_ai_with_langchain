Meridian sells three fare classes:

- Basic: cabin bag only, no changes, no refund.
- Flex: one checked bag included, date changes allowed for a fee, partial refund
  as travel credit.
- Premium: two checked bags, free changes, full refund up to 14 days before
  departure.

Fare rules are set at booking and shown on your confirmation.
