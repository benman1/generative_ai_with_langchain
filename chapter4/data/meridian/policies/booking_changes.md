You can change travel dates, names, or add extras from Manage My Booking.

Date changes are free on Premium fares, carry a change fee plus any fare
difference on Flex, and are not permitted on Basic. Name corrections of a few
characters are free; a full name change is treated as a new booking. Changes
within 48 hours of departure must be made by phone.
