Every Meridian package fare includes one cabin bag up to 8 kg.

Checked baggage is not included in the basic package fare. A 23 kg checked bag
costs 40 GBP each way when added in advance, or 55 GBP at the airport. A second
checked bag is 60 GBP. Sports and oversized items are priced on request.
