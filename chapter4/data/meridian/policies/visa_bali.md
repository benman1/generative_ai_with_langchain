Most visitors to Bali (including UK, US, Australian, and EU passport holders)
need a Visa on Arrival (VoA) for tourism, currently 500,000 IDR, valid for 30
days and extendable once. You can buy it on arrival or online as an e-VoA.

A passport valid for at least six months and a return ticket are required.
Confirm current fees and rules with the official Indonesian immigration source.
