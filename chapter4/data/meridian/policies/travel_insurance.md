The optional Meridian travel-insurance add-on covers medical emergencies and
repatriation, cancellation and curtailment for covered reasons, missed
departure, and lost or delayed baggage. Winter-sports and adventure activities
(including dog-sledding and glacier hikes) need the activity extension.

Cover is subject to the policy wording and excess. It does not cover
disinclination to travel. Read the policy document for exclusions.
