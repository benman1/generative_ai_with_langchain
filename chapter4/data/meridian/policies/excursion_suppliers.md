Meridian books excursions through vetted local suppliers:

- Iceland: Golden Circle coach tours, Blue Lagoon transfers, glacier hikes,
  whale watching from Reykjavik harbour.
- Norway (Oslo/Bergen): fjord sightseeing cruises, Flam railway, Holmenkollen.
- Tromso: northern-lights minibus chases, dog-sledding, winter whale safaris.
- Japan: Kyoto temple walking tours, tea-ceremony experiences.
- Bali: Mount Batur sunrise hike, Nusa islands snorkelling, Ubud rice terraces.

Excursions can be added at booking or in the app up to 48 hours before.
