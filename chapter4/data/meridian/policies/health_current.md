No COVID-19 vaccination or test is required for entry to any Meridian
destination as of March 2025.

For the Nordic destinations no special vaccinations are needed; routine UK/EU
vaccinations should be up to date. For Bali, hepatitis A and typhoid
vaccinations are commonly recommended; consider them well before travel. Tap
water is safe in the Nordic cities and Japan; in Bali drink bottled or filtered
water.

This is general guidance. Check the current advice for your nationality and
health with an official travel-health service before booking.
